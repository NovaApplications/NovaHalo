About NovaOS

NovaOS is a custom Android launcher workspace built on a modified foundation of the legacy, unmaintained Lawnchair Launcher (Version 1). We deliver a fast, classic, and highly customizable home screen experience through two distinct layouts: Nova Halo optimized for phones, and Nova Touch tailored specifically for tablets.
