About Nova Halo

Nova Halo and Nova Touch is a custom Android launcher built on a modified foundation of the legacy, unmaintained Lawnchair Launcher (Version 1). We deliver a fast, classic, and highly customizable home screen experience for Android users who appreciate simplicity and performance.
